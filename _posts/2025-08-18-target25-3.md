**D6. Smuggled away!**

**Question**
>Aye, ye be a smart one! Looks like we found where the stolen data is being smuggled out from... Since it looks like the pirates have already stolen some of our data, can we at least find out what they stole? If it's customer data, we may have an obligation to notify the affected parties...  
>
>Use your best skills to discover and what they took.  
>
>**Objectives**  
>
>Now that you can see where things are being exfiltrated, can you provide:  
>
>- the credit card's expiration date and CVV  
>- the email  
>
>of the poor soul (aka "gullible client") who we saw in the PCAP?  
>
>**Flag Format**  
>The flag is in the format:  
>CreditCardExpiration_CVVofCreditCard_email  
>
>**Required Tools**  
>
>- PCAP file from the previous challenge  
>- Wireshark or other packet analysis tool  
>- CyberChef or some coding language or skills to decode the data  
>
>**Additional Resources**  
>
>- [WireShark packet analysis tool](https://www.wireshark.org/)  
    - [Documentation and tutorials](https://www.wireshark.org/docs/)  
>- [tshark usage](https://tshark.dev/analyze/)  
>- [CyberChef en-/de-coder, slicer, dicer, multitool](https://gchq.github.io/CyberChef/)  
>- [Encoding formats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category:Binary-to-text_encoding_formats)  


**How I found the flag**
1. From the last challenge, I obtained the DNS packets.
![changed wireshark view setting](/assets/img/target2025/target2025_d6_1.png)
2. I tried extracting the packets as plain text.
![changed wireshark view setting](/assets/img/target2025/target2025_d6_2.png)
3. Also exported the packets to a CSV file. The contents were listed in time order, and I split the DNS names using the dot (.) as a delimiter.
![changed wireshark view setting](/assets/img/target2025/target2025_d6_3.png)
4. There were repeated domains. These looked suspicious, so I grabbed the subdomains, combined them, and tried to decode them, but I didn’t get the flag.
5. I grouped the subdomains by type (TXT, A, etc.) and also tried decoding. It didn’t work. I was stuck!
